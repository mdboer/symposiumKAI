# **Symposium Knowledge-driven AI in the era of genAI**

We are pleased to announce a symposium preceding the PhD defense of Roos Bakker! The symposium will be held on November 3rd, 10:00 - 13:00. The location is room 0.09 in the Huizinga Building (Johan Huizingagebouw, Doelensteeg 16, 2311 VL Leiden), in the centre of Leiden. Everyone is welcome and registration is not required. Lunch is NOT provided.
