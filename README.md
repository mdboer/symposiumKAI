# **Symposium Knowledge-driven AI in the era of genAI**

The symposium will be held on November 3rd, 10:00 - 13:00. The location is room 0.09 in the Huizinga Building (Johan Huizingagebouw, Doelensteeg 16, 2311 VL Leiden), in the centre of Leiden. Everyone is welcome and registration is not required. Lunch is NOT provided.





## Schedule



10:00 - 10:15 Opening Freek Bomhof (Program Director AI, TNO)

10:15 - 11:00 The RAIL Principles for Neurosymbolic AI: Reasoning, Assurances, Interfacing and Learning (Prof. Frank van Harmelen, VU)

11:00 - 11:45 Semantic Web for the Circular Economy: Challenges and Opportunities (Prof. Eva Blomqvist, Linköping University, Sweden)

11:45 - 12:30 From conversation to event-centric knowledge graphs for monitoring agents (Prof. Piek Vossen, VU)

12:30 - 12:45 Closing words
